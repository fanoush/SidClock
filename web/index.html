        The device talks to one host at a time and only accepts commands while Bluetooth access is R/W (shown on its main screen). It has no real-time clock of its own, so after a reset its clock is unset and otherwise drifts: the device card under the player shows both clocks and their offset, and the device time turns amber while they disagree - open the card and press set time to copy the browser clock over (the write carries milliseconds and compensates the Bluetooth delay, so both displays flip their seconds together). The volume slider drives the device's software volume, the same one its VOL slider shows. The HVSC section searches hvsc.c64.org and writes tunes straight to the device over SMP (song lengths and tune info come from the HVSC databases, cached by the browser) - that needs R/W mode too. Firmware updates use the same SMP link: the page checks the latest release when it connects and offers it when the device is older; keep the device on its charger and this tab open until the upload finishes.
