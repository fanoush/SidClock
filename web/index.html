The device talks to one host at a time and only accepts commands while Bluetooth access is R/W (shown on its main screen). It has no real-time clock of its own, so after a reset its clock is unset and otherwise drifts: the two clocks above show it, and the device one turns amber while they disagree - press set time to copy the browser clock over. All status values are pushed by the device; this page never polls.
