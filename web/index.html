        The device talks to one host at a time and only accepts commands while Bluetooth access is R/W (shown on its main screen). It has no real-time clock of its own, so after a reset its clock is unset and otherwise drifts: the two clocks above show it, and the device one turns amber while they disagree - press set time to copy the browser clock over. The HVSC section searches hvsc.c64.org and writes tunes straight to the device over SMP (song lengths and tune info come from the HVSC databases, cached by the browser) - that needs R/W mode too.
